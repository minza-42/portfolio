About me

Code, creativity
and curiosity

Hi! I'm a frontend student at Medieinstitutet in Sweden with a deep interest in creating modern, inclusive and aesthetically considered digital experiences.

My path here was not straight. After high school in animal care, I chose to follow a different passion and change direction completely. A decision that took courage but feels completely right.

Gaming and creativity have shaped who I am today. It sparked my interest in design, interactivity and how people experience digital products. What started as a passion for games grew into a love for frontend development and UX.

I thrive at the intersection of the technical and the creative. I don't just focus on making things work. I want to create experiences that feel natural, accessible and well-considered for every user.

As a person I'm curious, observant and hands-on. I learn best by testing, experimenting and solving real problems.
